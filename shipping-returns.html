Shipping & Returns

Pre-launch development policy: these terms are the planned V1 customer policy and will receive a final launch review before production checkout is enabled.

Shipping area

Version 1 is planned for shipping within the United States. Available carrier/service options and the shipping charge will be calculated from the delivery address and the actual package information for the item.

Tracked shipping

Orders will use tracked shipping. After a paid order is prepared and a label is created, tracking information will be associated with the order. Carrier transit estimates are estimates and are not guarantees of a specific delivery date.

Handling

The planned handling target is generally 1–3 business days after payment is confirmed. One-of-a-kind or unusually packaged items may require additional preparation, and any known delay should be communicated before shipment.

Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing a complete shipping address. Address changes should be requested before a shipping label is created; changes may not be possible after the package has entered carrier processing.

Returns

The planned V1 return window is 30 calendar days after confirmed delivery. Returned items should be sent back in materially the same condition in which they were received, including included accessories when applicable.

If an item arrives materially different from its listing description or damaged in transit, the customer should contact MayIonics before returning it so the issue can be reviewed. For other approved returns, return-shipping responsibility may depend on the reason for return and will be shown in the final launch policy.
