About the marketplace

About MayIonics

MayIonics is an independent resale marketplace for quality new, open-box, and pre-owned finds sourced from discount stores, garage sales, closeouts, and other value opportunities.

How inventory works

Inventory changes frequently and many listings may be one-of-a-kind. Each item is evaluated individually before it is listed, and the product page is intended to show the actual condition, price, availability, and shipping information for that item.

Condition transparency

MayIonics uses clear condition labels such as New, Open Box, and Pre-Owned. Material wear, missing accessories, packaging differences, or other important condition details should be disclosed in the individual listing rather than hidden behind generic marketing language.

Independent seller history

MayIonics is a new storefront, but the seller has prior eBay selling history. Historical feedback is shown separately on the Seller Reviews page and can be verified through the public eBay profile.

Contact

A dedicated MayIonics customer contact method will be published before production commerce is activated. No placeholder email address is published during development.
