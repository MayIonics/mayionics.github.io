# P12 Reviews and Policy Content Plan

## Goal

Complete the non-commerce trust/content layer for the MayIonics storefront while keeping production commerce inactive.

## Scope

1. Replace the Seller Reviews placeholder with clearly labeled historical eBay seller feedback from the supplied eBay feedback record.
2. Link prominently to the public eBay feedback profile for source verification.
3. Update About with the MayIonics independent resale model and condition-transparency approach.
4. Update Shipping & Returns with a clear development policy for U.S. tracked shipping and returns, marked for final launch review before production activation.
5. Add Privacy and Terms pages and link them from the storefront footer.
6. Keep product/payment/shipping runtime behavior unchanged.

## Content boundaries

- Historical eBay feedback must never be presented as MayIonics website purchase reviews.
- Do not import or reuse NutriLeaf/Etsy product reviews for MayIonics.
- Do not publish private reviewer/order data beyond short feedback text already visible in the supplied public eBay feedback record.
- Policy language remains a pre-launch draft until P14 production review.
- No provider, Cloudflare, D1, payment, shipping, or production setting change.
