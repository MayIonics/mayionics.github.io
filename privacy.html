Pre-launch policy

Privacy Policy

This development policy describes the information MayIonics expects to use for Version 1 commerce. It will receive a final launch review before production checkout is enabled.

Information used to fulfill orders

MayIonics may process customer name, email address, shipping address, selected shipping service, order contents, order totals, provider transaction identifiers, shipment tracking information, and communications needed to support an order.

Payment information

Card payments are planned to be processed by Stripe and PayPal payments by PayPal. MayIonics does not store full card numbers or PayPal account credentials in its storefront or D1 database. Payment providers handle the sensitive payment credentials required for their services.

Shopping cart storage

The storefront uses browser localStorage (local storage) for convenience cart state. The cart stores product identifiers and quantities only; it is not authoritative for price, stock, shipping cost, or payment status.

Service providers

MayIonics plans to use Cloudflare for backend/data services, Stripe and PayPal for payments, and EasyPost for shipping functions. Information necessary to complete those services may be sent to the relevant provider when commerce is activated.

Data use

Order information is intended to be used for checkout, fraud and error prevention, customer support, fulfillment, accounting/audit records, refunds when applicable, and legal obligations. MayIonics does not plan to sell customer personal information to advertisers.

Retention and security

Commerce records may be retained as reasonably needed for order history, accounting, dispute handling, fraud prevention, and legal requirements. Access to administrative order data is designed to be restricted through server-side authorization.

Contact

A dedicated privacy/contact channel will be published before production commerce is activated.
