Pre-launch policy

Terms of Sale

These development terms describe the planned Version 1 sales rules and will receive a final review before live commerce is activated.

Product condition and descriptions

MayIonics sells new, open-box, and pre-owned items. Customers should review the individual listing for condition, included accessories, packaging details, known wear, and other item-specific information before purchase.

Availability

Many items may be one-of-a-kind or limited quantity. An item shown on the site is not guaranteed available until the server confirms inventory during checkout and payment is successfully reconciled.

Pricing and payment

The authoritative price is the price stored by MayIonics at checkout, not a value supplied or modified in the browser. Version 1 is planned to accept supported Stripe card payments and PayPal. An order is not considered paid merely because a checkout session or provider order was created.

Shipping

Shipping options and charges are based on the delivery destination and package information. Carrier delivery estimates are not guaranteed. Customers are responsible for providing an accurate deliverable address.

Returns

The planned return policy is described on the Shipping & Returns page. Return eligibility may depend on timing, item condition, and the reason for return. Final return terms will be confirmed before production checkout is enabled.

Order cancellation and errors

MayIonics may cancel or correct an order when inventory is unavailable, a listing contains a material error, payment cannot be verified, fraud or abuse is suspected, or fulfillment cannot reasonably be completed. Any required refund would be handled through the original payment method when the refund system is active.

Site status

MayIonics is currently in development. Test/Sandbox payment, shipping, and backend components described by the project are not an offer to conduct live commerce until production activation is explicitly completed.

Contact

A dedicated customer contact method will be published before launch.
